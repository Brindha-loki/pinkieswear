Shipping & Refund Policy

Thank you for shopping with us. Every nail set is carefully handcrafted and prepared specifically for your order. Please read our Shipping & Refund Policy before placing an order.

Shipping Policy

  • All nail sets are handmade after your order is confirmed.
  • Estimated delivery time is 10–20 business days, depending on your location and courier services.
  • You will be able to track your order status directly from your account as it progresses through different stages.
  • The order status shown on your account is updated by our admin team and reflects the current stage of your order.
  • If your package has not been delivered at all, please contact us immediately for assistance.

Order Status

Your order may pass through various stages such as:

  • Order Placed
  • Confirmed
  • Preparation Started
  • Packed
  • Shipped

These statuses are updated by our admin team and will automatically appear in your customer account.

Cancellation Policy

  • Orders may be cancelled only before preparation begins.
  • Once the order status changes to "Preparation Started", cancellation is no longer permitted.
  • As every nail set is custom-made, no refund will be provided for cancelled orders after preparation has started.

Refund Policy

  • Refunds are only considered for products that arrive damaged during shipping.
  • An uninterrupted opening/unboxing video is mandatory for every damage claim.
  • The video must clearly show the sealed package being opened for the first time.
  • Claims submitted without an opening video cannot be approved.
  • If the claim is verified, an appropriate refund or replacement will be processed at our discretion.
  • No refunds will be issued for:
    • Order cancellations after preparation has started.
    • Change of mind.
    • Incorrect sizing provided by the customer.
    • Normal wear and tear after use.

    • Incorrect Product Received

    • If you receive a nail set that is different from the one you ordered, you may be eligible for a refund or replacement.
    • You must provide clear image proof showing the incorrect product received along with your order details.
    • Once our team verifies the issue, we will process an appropriate refund or replacement.
    • Claims made without sufficient image proof may not be approved.

    Return Policy

    • For hygiene reasons, we do not accept returns on any nail sets.
    • Please ensure you review your order carefully before placing it.
    • If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please refer to the Refund Policy above for the applicable claim process.
